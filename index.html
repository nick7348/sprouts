About Us

A Place Where Every Child Thrives

At Sprouts Preschool and Kindergarten in Hosur, we blend the best of Montessori philosophy, Reggio Emilia influence, and STEM integration — aligned with NEP 2020 & NCF 2023 — to nurture the whole child: physically, emotionally, cognitively, and spiritually.

Our approach is rooted in play, exploration, and love — giving every young learner a joyful foundation for lifelong success.

NEP 2020 & NCF 2023 aligned, blending Montessori with modern STEM exploration.

✅
Safe & Child-Friendly

Hygienic, child-friendly campus with a dedicated outdoor play area.
